# Rental Manager

Terms used to describe rent tracking and family income allocation.

## Language

**Allocation**:
The intended division of received income between family members. A recorded allocation does not establish that the money has been distributed.
_Avoid_: Payout, transfer (when referring to the calculation).

**Payout**:
The actual delivery or transfer of money to a family member following an allocation.
_Avoid_: Allocation (when referring to money actually distributed).

**Equal-share allocation**:
A division of the distributable amount into three equal shares for the owner, her husband, and her daughter. These are thirds of the whole, not literal 33% shares.

**Apartment expense**:
A cost related to an apartment, borne equally by the owner, her husband, and her daughter.

**Distributable amount**:
Received rent less apartment expenses included in the same summary. It excludes rent that is owed but has not been received.

**Outstanding rent**:
The unpaid portion of rent owed by a tenant. It is separate from family income allocation.

**Rental month**:
The month to which rent and its financial summary belong. A late receipt updates the rental month it settles while retaining the date the money was actually received.
