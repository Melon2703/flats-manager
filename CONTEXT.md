# Flats Manager (Rental Back Office)

Digital rental management back-office system tailored for Anya's mom (~10 flats), replacing her paper notebook via Telegram Bot and Telegram Web App (TWA).

## Language

**Flat**:
A physical rental apartment managed in the system.
_Avoid_: Apartment, unit, property, room

**Tenancy**:
A distinct rental lease period for a specific Flat, defining start/end dates, monthly rent, deposit amount, payment due day, contract documents, and move-in/out checklists.
_Avoid_: Lease agreement, contract, rental agreement, booking

**Tenant**:
An individual or group renting a Flat under an active or historical Tenancy.
_Avoid_: Client, renter, occupant

**Expected Payment**:
A scheduled monthly rent obligation generated automatically for a Tenancy based on its payment due day.
_Avoid_: Bill, invoice, charge

**Payment Record**:
A ledger entry recording an actual transaction (full/partial payment, cash or bank transfer) tied to an Expected Payment or deposit.
_Avoid_: Receipt entry, invoice payout

**Reminder Draft**:
A pre-formatted polite message text generated by the system for Anya's mom to manually copy and send to a Tenant.
_Avoid_: Automated message, notification template

**Forward Capture**:
The process of receiving forwarded messages, photos, voice notes, or documents in Telegram and assigning them to a Flat with a category tag.
_Avoid_: File upload, media import

**Timeline Event**:
A chronological record entry (payment receipt, maintenance expense, voice note transcript, checklist photo, or note) attached to a Flat or Tenancy.
_Avoid_: Log item, history record

**Inspection Checklist**:
A structured inventory of condition items, appliance states, photos, and meter readings recorded at move-in or move-out.
_Avoid_: Room inventory, condition report

**Final Settlement**:
The calculated end-of-tenancy financial reconciliation determining deposit refund or balance due after accounting for unpaid rent, utilities, cleaning fees, and damage deductions.
_Avoid_: Security deposit payout, move-out invoice

**Weekly Snapshot Digest**:
A periodic summary report sent via Telegram Bot containing overdue payments, upcoming lease transitions, missing documentation, and monthly collection status.
_Avoid_: Weekly email, status report

**Actionable Alert**:
A real-time Telegram notification sent for high-priority operational triggers (e.g. overdue rent or expiring tenancy) containing inline action buttons.
_Avoid_: Push notification, automated ping
