# 05: Correct financial entries with history

**What to build:** Correct or remove erroneous receipts and expenses, including entries assigned to the wrong rental month. Recalculate affected monthly figures and retain understandable history of meaningful corrections through the same simple financial workflow.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Record expenses and see family shares; 04 — Record late receipts and known-month advances.

**Status:** Approved breakdown; unpublished draft.

- [ ] The owner can correct the amount, actual date, or assigned rental month of a receipt or expense without creating a second active entry for the same corrected record.
- [ ] An erroneous receipt or expense can be removed from active totals while meaningful correction history remains available.
- [ ] Correcting an amount updates received rent or expenses, outstanding rent where applicable, and applicable family shares.
- [ ] Moving an entry removes its effect from the wrong month and applies it once to the correct month.
- [ ] Changing an actual transaction date alone does not silently change its assigned rental month.
- [ ] Unrelated months and apartments remain unchanged.
- [ ] After reopening, both corrected totals and meaningful history are available.
- [ ] Mobile-browser acceptance checks cover receipt and expense corrections, removal, and wrong-month corrections with visible before/after totals.

Use ordinary allocation examples whose shares divide exactly. This ticket does not resolve allocation remainder, negative-share, security-deposit, or exceptional-allocation rules.
